ABOUT US

Meet the team behind INTROSPHEER

INTROSPHEER is made to help people create meaningful social bonds through shared interests, events and conversation. We aim to build a welcoming environment where users can find things to do, meet others and feel more willing to get involved.

OUR TEAM

Built together.

INTROSPHEER was developed as a collaborative effort of our team for our university project. We worked together to create an experience that makes social interaction easier and more approachable through research, design, prototyping, testing and development.

Every team member brought different ideas, perspectives and skills to the design and development process.
