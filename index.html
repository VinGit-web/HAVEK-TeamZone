ABOUT US

Meet the team behind INTROSPHEER

INTROSPHEER is designed to help people create meaningful social connections through shared interests, local events and conversation. The platform provides a space where users can discover activities, build connections and become more involved in their local community.

OUR TEAM

Built together.

INTROSPHEER was developed collaboratively as part of our university project. Our team worked across research, design, prototyping, user testing and development to create an approachable social experience.

Each team member contributed different ideas, perspectives and technical skills throughout the development process.
