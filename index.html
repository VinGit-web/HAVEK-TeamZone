ABOUT US

Meet the team behind INTROSPHEER

INTROSPHEER is made to help people create meaningful social bonds through shared interests, events and conversation. We aim to build a welcoming environment where users can find things to do, meet others and feel more willing to get involved.

OUR TEAM

Built together.

INTROSPHEER was developed as a collaborative university project focused on making social interaction easier and more approachable.

Our team worked across research, design, prototyping, testing and development to create the final experience.

Every team member contributed different ideas, perspectives and skills throughout the design process.
